# Code of conduct

HouseCheck exists to help renters, many of whom are dealing with a stressful housing
situation. Treat everyone here, contributors and users alike, with the same care.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Back technical claims with evidence.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, including basic ones.
- Respect privacy. Don't post anyone's personal information, including tenants' or
  landlords' details beyond what the public record already shows.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at **ness.aisling@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove anyone who breaks this code, in
issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
